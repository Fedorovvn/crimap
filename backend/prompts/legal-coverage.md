Inspect every supplied incident-related paragraph, including the closing police/court qualification. For EVERY supported suspect/convicted participant, either supply all supported catalog mappings or explain exactly what is missing. An unnamed perpetrator explicitly described by the source is still a supported participant; do not omit that person solely because the name is unknown. Never invent a suspect for an accidental injury or assign liability to a victim.

Do not wait for a numeric section or a final judgment: an explicitly reported suspected offense is enough for a matching catalog item with its conditions. Source attribution, possible qualification, current procedural stage and guilt are separate. Source text quoting police in a newspaper remains a media source. Check age, attempt/completion, intent/negligence and aggravating circumstances; do not pick the harshest or easiest available range. Never sum ranges or predict the sentence.

If no legal label is reported, examine whether a catalog item's factBasedMapping explicitly allows a conditional comparison. Only those entries permit qualification=possible, statuteMatch=editorial. Meet EVERY required factual element with exact quotations; preserve the catalog's statutory data and exclusions. Do not use the generic ban on model personal hypotheses to suppress this explicitly allowed conditional legal comparison. No memory-based sections/ranges, no inference from the headline/category alone. If factual elements are missing or conflicting, explain the missing elements instead of guessing.

In the details stage keep coverage.legal=mapped for nonempty legal, otherwise the appropriate existing coverage code with a concrete Russian reason; in no-reported-qualification explain why a conditional mapping is also unavailable. For a known offense with no verified catalog match, also propose a legal.catalog.<name> request with the original qualification and exact quote. A missing catalog item is NOT evidence of no legal consequences.

In FINAL PRO REVIEW, always return legalCoverage {status,reason,participants:[{participantKey,status,reason}]}. Reasons are concise Russian explanations; participants includes EVERY final suspect/convicted participant exactly once, and no victims. Statuses: mapped; partial (overall only, when some suspects are mapped and others not); insufficient-facts; no-catalog-match; no-suspect (overall only); not-applicable. Describe the missing fact (e.g. intent, injury severity, juvenile rules), not merely "нет данных". For not-applicable include basis:[{documentId,quote}] with exact supplied official-source quotations in that report item. not-applicable requires explicit supporting official evidence that criminal/administrative consequences do not apply; silence, unresolved investigation or a deceased victim is NOT that evidence. Prefer "доступных сведений недостаточно" over "юридических последствий нет". For mapped items explain whether they are reported or conditional and what range was mapped. Check this yourself against sources, apply corrections and fill omissions before passing; do not merely echo Flash's coverage. Coverage describes the FINAL corrected event, not the incoming draft.
