EDITORIAL SCOPE — apply to the actual incident, before generic danger/novelty keywords.

Crime Map covers sudden personal danger during ordinary life in Budapest: being attacked, threatened with a weapon, robbed, seriously hit by a vehicle, or experiencing a home intrusion. Domestic violence and attacks by acquaintances/partners are in scope; the attacker need not be a stranger. Keep unusual dangerous street/transit incidents and serious or potentially serious road accidents. The user's rejection of a single event is NOT a new category ban.

OUT OF SCOPE:
- Animal-centered incidents of any severity: bites/attacks, rescues, cruelty, veterinary stories, or incidents principally about animals. An animal merely mentioned in an independent human crime (e.g. a person stabbed while walking their dog) does not change that crime's relevance.
- Industrial/chemical/environmental disaster coverage, including a factory chlorine leak with hospitalizations or evacuation. Numbers of hospitalized people do not override this exclusion. Do not generalize this into a ban on ordinary residential fires, domestic carbon-monoxide accidents, intentional attacks using chemicals, or large road crashes.
- Drug dealing, possession, consumption, seizures and drug raids as the main event. A drug-related detail does not exclude a stabbing, robbery, assault on a bystander or a crash harming others. Do not assume people voluntarily took drugs just because they became ill at a festival; unknown cause stays unknown.
- Police raids, searches, patrol/checkpoint summaries, speeding fines, weapon possession/seizure, prison contraband, court/prison administration, or an arrest alone, with no separate in-scope incident. Arrests and court developments about a concrete in-scope assault/homicide remain useful updates to that incident.
- Economic/corruption/procurement/fraud cases and business disputes without a separate physical attack or coercive home intrusion. Do not relabel nonviolent theft/fraud as robbery to make it relevant. Minor property-only theft/damage, restaurant nonpayment and ordinary unattended-car theft are not the core feed; violent robbery and residential burglary remain in scope.
- Accidents whose main event is voluntary extreme recreation/stunts (paragliding, skydiving, similar activities), with harm confined to participants. Retain separate serious harm or concrete danger inflicted on uninvolved people. Ordinary walking, travelling, work, relationships, intoxication or a victim's carelessness must NOT be treated as consenting to violence or injury. Do not infer criminal participation or voluntary risk without evidence.

BORDERLINE ROAD ACCIDENTS: keep for editorial choice when potentially consequential, even if exact injury severity is unclear. In particular an overturned ambulance with two medics, two patients and another injured person remains eligible. The editor rejected one such story by personal preference, not a general rule. Existing minor-collision filtering still applies only to clearly minor cases.

Words such as weapon, wanted, rescue, evacuation, hospital, shocking or unusual do not by themselves establish relevance. Inspect the main action, people harmed/threatened, context and consequences. Read only the relevant article; ignore adverts, related-story links and unrelated historical anecdotes. Mixed articles can contain both excluded and included events: retain only included events. For triage return keep=true if there is any concrete in-scope incident. For identify/extract omit out-of-scope incidents and allow an empty result.
